Delete Your Account & Data

You can permanently delete your Fast Fix Work account and personal data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Fast Fix Work app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account (below "Sign out").
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll process the deletion within 30 days.

Send to support@fastfixwork.com with the subject "Delete my account" and include the name and phone number on your account so we can verify it.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile (name, email, phone, photo, bio)
  • Your job postings / applications and chat messages
  • Reviews and ratings tied to your account
  • Push notification tokens

What we keep, and for how long

Completed transaction & payout recordsRetained up to 7 years for legal, tax, and accounting compliance, then deleted.
Everything elseDeleted immediately (in-app) or within 30 days (email request).
