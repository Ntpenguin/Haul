FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to code anything?

No. Setup is entirely no-code: pick an industry template, describe your business, choose a voice, set business hours, and import a knowledge base from a website URL or PDF. The agent is configured from the dashboard.

What does it actually cost?

Because you run it on your own infrastructure (Docker + Postgres), you pay the underlying providers at cost — roughly $0.04–0.10 per minute across telephony, speech-to-text, the LLM, and voice — instead of a marked-up per-minute SaaS rate. No surprise platform fees.

Which phone provider does it use?

It's provider-agnostic. Out of the box it works with Twilio for telephony, Deepgram for transcription, OpenAI or Anthropic for the brain, and ElevenLabs for voice. You can buy numbers in the dashboard or bring your own — and there's a keyless demo mode to try it first.

Can I resell it to my own clients?

Yes — it's multi-tenant SaaS by design. You get self-serve tenant signup, per-tenant isolation, Stripe subscriptions with metered per-minute billing, full white-label branding, and in-dashboard number purchasing.

Is it really self-hosted?

Completely. It runs on your infrastructure via Docker and Postgres, so your call data, recordings, and customer records stay under your control. Nothing is locked to a vendor.

What about call recording consent?

Recording and transcription are configurable per business, including a customizable consent announcement at the start of calls. You're responsible for complying with the recording-consent laws in your jurisdiction, and the platform gives you the controls to do so.
